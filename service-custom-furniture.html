Our Process

From Sketch to Installation

01

Site Measurement

Precise measurements at site, existing constraints noted, plumbing / electrical points marked.

02

Design & 3D

Elevation drawings, storage plan and photo-real 3D of each piece for approval.

03

Material & Hardware Selection

Physical samples of laminates, veneers, edgebands, handles and drawer systems.

04

Factory Manufacturing

Cutting, edge-banding, drilling and pre-assembly done in factory for precision.

05

On-Site Installation

Delivered flat-packed and installed on site with minimal mess and no rework.

06

Handover & Warranty

Final adjustments, cleaning, handover with warranty on hardware and ply.
